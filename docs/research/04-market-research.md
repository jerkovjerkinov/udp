# Market and research findings (verified core)

The coding thread's preliminary sweeps are in `coding-preliminary-sweeps.json` (59 items). This file keeps the facts that drive the design. Items marked ✔ were checked against a source in this pass. The rest come from the sweeps and are plausible but unverified.

## What sells and retains in 3D / spatial puzzles
- ✔ **2D → 3D with orbit lifts retention.** Screw puzzles more than doubled revenue to over $206M in 2025. Screwdom holds about 45% of that, and its US D30 is about 17% (estimate), against about 8% for Magic Sort! and 4% for Color Block Jam. Naavik attributes this mainly to the move to 3D with camera rotation. [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/how-niche-subgenres-are-reshaping-the-mobile-puzzle-market/)
- Tap Away (50M+ installs) and Parking Jam 3D (100M+) show that one-tap 3D dismantling works on phones. These figures are from the sweeps.
- ✔ **Architecture as theme sells.**
  - Monument Valley: 26M downloads and $14.4M in 2 years (21M of those downloads during free promotions). [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/63216/monument-valley-2-year-revenue/)
  - Tiny Glade: about 616K copies in its first month at $15, with 1.37M wishlists at launch. Its design pillars were "a lot from little effort", "no wrong answers" and "it's alive". [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-tiny-glade-built-its-way-to-600k)
  - Blue Prince (floor-plan drafting): 2M+ players, including Game Pass and PS Plus. [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/tech-news/mustafa-mahmoud/indie-puzzler-blue-prince-surpasses-2-million-total-players/)
- ✔ **Drawing becomes space wins awards.** Viewfinder won BAFTA 2024 British Game and New IP. [BAFTA](https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/20th-bafta-games-awards-winners)
- ✔ **Projection as a premium mechanic.** Shadowmatic: Apple Design Award 2015; 70 levels in 9 themed rooms in the base game; graded hints with an "advise against" stance. It overlaps the shadow game, so treat it as evidence only. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadowmatic)
- ✔ **Voxel clue puzzles with stars, strikes and a reveal.** Picross 3D: Metacritic 83; 3 stars by time and strikes; 5 strikes and you restart; bonus unlocks. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picross_3D)
- ✔ **Mastery stars.** Euclidea: 4.8★; separate stars for the minimum number of lines and of elementary constructions; levels unlock in sequence. [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/927914361)
- Paper Fold: 4.6★ from 343K ratings (sweeps). Folding is a validated mass-market verb.
- Wordle 5.3B plays and NYT 11.1B plays in 2024 (sweeps). The daily format is the cheapest retention feature.

## Learning science
- **Training works, lasts and transfers.** Uttal et al. 2013: g ≈ 0.47 over 206 studies, durable, with transfer. This is a widely cited figure; PubMed was not reachable in this pass.
- ✔ **Architecture-specific skills.** Berkowitz et al. 2021 (ETH, N = 593 cross-sectional, 117 longitudinal):
  - master's versus beginning students differ most in **mental cutting** (g = 0.68), then indoor **perspective taking** (g = 0.43) and **object composition/packing** (g = 0.32);
  - **mental rotation** shows no difference (g = 0.11);
  - in year 1, packing, cutting and rotation improve, but perspective taking does not.
  - Design implication: aim at cutting/section thinking (Origamic), composition (Build Order, Stack) and perspective (an untrained gap) rather than pure rotation. [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/article/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.609363)
- ✔ **Sorby's sketching course** improved 3D visualisation, grades and retention in engineering majors. A UCSD iPad app pilot (sketch on a grid with instant feedback) helped more than half of the struggling students within about 3 weeks; it had no control group. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2013/09/130916103526.htm)
- **Commit, then reveal** (Cohen & Hegarty cross-section training; Spatial Vis; Click the Cube charges stars for peeking). Every game requires a committed answer before the 3D reveal. From the sweeps.

## Gaps (nobody does these)
- **Origamic architecture**: no game or app found, only research tools and craft books. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origamic_architecture)
- **Non-cube developments**: cube-net apps stop at cubes, while Sorby, the PAT and Spatial Vis train prisms, wedges and pyramids.
- **Construction order / exploded axo** applied to buildings: the Tap Away loop has never been used on tectonics. A search found only generic nuts-and-bolts and demolition games.
- **Architectural rules as the puzzle**, not just the theme: Tiny Glade, Townscaper and Monument Valley never test the rules they show.
